SENTINELS — FERRY SEQUENCE
Condensed video-direction brief

REFERENCE MAP
- Master image: exact opening composition, ferry geography, Axis C, subject scale and character placement.
- Individual character sheets: identity, costume and appearance for UV, Shuki, Nishi, Sunil and Badri.
- Ferry environment sheet: boat design and painterly environment language.

VISUAL LANGUAGE
2D cinematic anime. Clean cel-shaded characters inside a painterly ferry environment. Night lighting with a cold blue tone and readable dramatic expressions.

CONTINUITY RULES
- Maintain Axis C: the established viewing side of the scene.
- Crimson mist remains overhead and moves from the foreground toward the back of the ferry—never sideways or toward camera.
- Strong wind moves clothing and hair; the boat sways subtly with the waves.
- Preserve character identity, costume, scale, stable linework and background continuity.
- Five characters appear only in the opening shot block. No extras.

EMOTIONAL ARC
Panic → recognition → dread → protective concern → fading consciousness.

TIMED STORY BEATS

SHOT 04 — 00:00–00:03
Start on the wide, high-angle master composition. UV thrashes at the centre while Shuki, Nishi, Sunil and Badri tighten around him. Cut within the same block to a tight UV close-up: scared, erratic and only partly conscious.

SHOT 07 — 00:03–00:05
Medium close-up favoring Badri. He studies UV below, then slowly lifts his gaze toward the passing mist. Static camera; alarm turns into recognition.

SHOT 08 — 00:05–00:07
Close-up favoring Shuki with shallow depth of field. She turns from UV and looks upward. UV stays behind her, soft and nearly unconscious. Tension becomes dread.

SHOT 10 — 00:07–00:09
Continue on Shuki. She snaps her attention back down toward UV. Protective concern overtakes the horror of what she has seen.

SHOT 11 — 00:09–00:11
Tight static close-up on UV. His eyelids grow heavy, his breath becomes shallow and the last trace of panic drains from his face as he slips toward unconsciousness.

QUALITY TARGETS
No identity swaps, extra characters, unstable subject scale, palette flicker, line boil, shape warping or background instability.
